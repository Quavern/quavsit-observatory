## Preamble

This preamble explains why Quavern publishes under this licence. It is not a
condition of the licence and does not limit any right granted below.

Quavern publishes software so that it can be inspected, reused and maintained
for the long run. Changes to the files we publish should stay open for the
people who use them, including when the software is only reached over a
network; the rest of a larger program remains its author's choice.

Much of what we publish handles open data. Its producers ask to be credited,
and this licence keeps their credit attached to the software.

Quavern does not build generators of images, video or music, because those
systems are mostly trained on the work of artists without their consent. We
ask everyone who uses our software to respect the people whose work it
processes. That request is a position, not a restriction: the licence grants
its rights for every purpose.
